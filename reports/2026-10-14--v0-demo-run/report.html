  • High-risk. fmt's 7 in run B were write_buffer removed and added, fill_n added, and 4 of the edges nested under them. Its 5 now are the three top-level change events on write_buffer, one of its callers (print, 0.61), and fill_n added. spdlog's 36 in run B were 22 NodeAdded (11 of them the re-added functions), 11 NodeRemoved, 2 NodeBodyChanged and 1 EdgeAdded. Its 35 now are 11 NodeSignatureChanged, 11 NodeAttrChanged(default_arguments) on the same 11 functions, 11 NodeAdded and 2 NodeBodyChanged. Without ADR-046's gate it would be 43.
  • Top-level and components. spdlog's top-level count doubled (59 to 122) and its components nearly tripled (17 to 50). (inferred) In run B a removed function's edges were change events nested under it, and the edges of the 11 split functions joined them into one large component. Paired, those edges are not change events, so the functions fall into separate components; and the 29 sugared_signature and 19 macro change events are top-level, with nothing to nest under.
  • Model size. Fewer entities match (fmt 10,718 to 8,809, spdlog 2,466 to 1,032) and fewer edges are counted (fmt 26,458 to 18,964, spdlog 5,345 to 2,579). (inferred) googletest and bundled fmt are no longer modelled: their entities and internal edges are gone, and fmt's calls into googletest now leave the model, which is why fmt's share of edges leaving it rose from 19.6% to 41.2%. fmt's TUs went from 51 to 50: one compile-database entry is third-party and not parsed.
  • Unmatched. No base-side entity is left unmatched in either repository (run B: 1 and 11). The head-side ones left are additions.
  • Time. The cold pipeline took longer (fmt 170.5 to 195.8 s, spdlog 21.9 to 27.4 s), nearly all of it in produce. One run of each on shared runners, so this is not a measured regression, and the artifacts do not say why. The new stages cost under 1 s on fmt.

4 · Observations

  1. One signature change is still several high-risk change events. No cause rule links a NodeAttrChanged(default_arguments) or a NodeBodyChanged to the NodeSignatureChanged on the same function, so each is top-level. Adding a defaulted parameter changes all three: fmt's one edit is 3 top-level high-risk change events (4 with a caller), and spdlog's 11 edits are 22 of its 35 high-risk ones.
  2. spdlog's ranking opens on spelling. ADR-046 changes the count, not the order, as it says. Rank 1, and the head of component 0, is a spelling-only default argument (total 0.73, impact 0.86); rank 3 is the other. 11 of component 0's 20 change events are spelling only, and its first high-risk change event is at rank 39. The first high-risk change event overall, basic_logger_mt's signature change, is rank 2.
  3. Where spdlog's changed lines go. 1,219 of its 1,487 changed C++ lines are on no change event's lines, and the tool puts about 828 of them in files no model declares anything in, such as tests/test_file_logging.cpp and include/spdlog/sinks/loki_sink.h (8 TUs model this repository). fmt has 18 such lines, in 3 files that are all modelled: lines in format-test.cc and compile-test.cc, and the core.h pragma line the commit that entered the range changed.
  4. No bundled-fmt change events, and none expected. Run B's spdlog document had none under include/spdlog/fmt/bundled either; the path did not change in either range. With the option, 166 and 167 relationships into it are recorded as crossings, and the tool listed no other path as looking third-party.
  5. The warm rerun is fast. fmt 16.5 s and spdlog 1.5 s in-process, against 195.8 s and 27.4 s cold, and the warm terminal views print no invalidation note. The warm runs' documents are not kept, so their stage timings are not known.
