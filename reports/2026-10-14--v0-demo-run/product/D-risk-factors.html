D · Why each change event ranks where it does

Each top-level change event's score is a weighted sum of four factors. The bar shows each factor's contribution, and its length is the ranking total. High-risk is decided separately, by impact: the first three factors only, rescaled to 0–1, at or above 0.6, and never for a change of spelling only (ADR-046). That is why the threshold is not drawn as a line on this axis. A spelling-only change event keeps its place in the order and is marked spelling only.
